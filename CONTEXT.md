# Site Safety Forms

Framers record daily site safety checks and supporting photos. Admins review these submissions across workers, sites, and dates.

## Language

**Framer**:
A worker who submits safety forms and can view their own submissions.
_Avoid_: Customer, member

**Admin**:
A user who can view submissions from all framers.
_Avoid_: Approver (approval authority has not been defined)

**Job site**:
A named work location associated with a safety submission.
_Avoid_: Project (which could mean the software project)

**Submission**:
A framer's safety form for a selected job site and date, including checklist answers, notes, and supporting photos.
_Avoid_: Report (which could mean an aggregate summary)

**Checklist answer**:
A framer's assessment of one safety item as Pass, Issue, or Not applicable.

**Issue**:
A reported safety concern accompanied by explanatory notes. Reporting an issue does not prevent a submission.
_Avoid_: Validation error

**Submitted**:
The state of a completed safety form recorded for review; it does not certify that the site is safe.

**Work date**:
The calendar date the safety form describes, interpreted in the America/Vancouver business timezone. It is distinct from when the submission was recorded.
_Avoid_: Submission timestamp

**Supporting photo**:
An image attached to a submission, available to its framer and to admins.
