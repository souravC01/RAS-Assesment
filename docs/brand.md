# Assessment branding provenance

- Official website: [Ron Anderson & Sons Ltd.](https://www.rasltd.ca/), retrieved October 3, 2026.
- App logo: the site's green RGB logo, copied without alteration to `frontend/public/ras-logo.png` from [the official Squarespace-hosted asset](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/603d792c9b2ff375c0f46cb9/8db992b5-db8b-413d-9da1-005016d0b9e0/RAS+Logo_Updated+May+2023_RGB_green.png).
- The logo's dominant opaque green pixel is RGB (13, 85, 58), `#0d553a`. Interface green is `#045339`, matching the official website's surfaces and actions, verified October 5, 2026.
- Charcoal `#2a2829`, squared controls, and Gainsborough Sans display lettering follow the official website. Light working surfaces and warning colors are application choices.
- Official website cross-links [@rasltdframing on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/rasltdframing/). Its identity is established by that cross-link; the direct Instagram fetch failed, so no claims are made about its current content.
- The app's charcoal header renders the existing transparent logo white using CSS; no additional logo asset is needed.
- Gainsborough Sans is stored at `frontend/public/fonts/gainsborough-sans.otf` from [the font source in the supplied design reference](https://file.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v2/namespaces/fonts/libraries/603d792c9b2ff375c0f46cb9/assets/e3ab944a-c588-48c1-a494-7d9bd7d4b7fd/font.otf). The live website uses it for headings, navigation, and actions; body text remains a system sans-serif for readable forms and records.
- The user-approved visual update excludes decorative construction imagery. Supporting photos remain part of safety submissions.
